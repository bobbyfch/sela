A few things about this room

Books are stored locally in IndexedDB. No account, upload, or tracking. Removing the extension or clearing its data removes the shelf: keep your original files and export reading notes.

Want this room whenever you open a tab? Install the separate New Tab edition. The standard edition leaves your browser's new tab unchanged.

PDF, EPUB, CBZ and text. Narration uses available browser/OS voices. Documents with text can be searched; scanned PDFs need OCR. Mobile browser extension capabilities vary.
