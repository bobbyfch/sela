A few things about this room

Books are stored locally in IndexedDB. No account, upload, or tracking. Removing the extension or clearing its data removes the shelf: keep your original files and export reading notes.

Sela Home is the desktop new-tab product. Choose its New Tab package. Standard is a compatibility edition and does not replace new tabs.

PDF, EPUB, CBZ and text. Narration uses available browser/OS voices. Documents with text can be searched; scanned PDFs need OCR. Home is for desktop; Bookshelf is the mobile app.
