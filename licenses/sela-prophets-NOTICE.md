# Kisah 25 Nabi — SELA editorial pack

The narrative summaries, notes and accuracy statements in `library/packs/prophets-id-v1.json` are original
text written for SELA and are released under the repository's MIT licence.

The pack cites Quran passages by surah and verse number only. It does not copy any Quran text or any
published translation. When a reader opens a citation, Sela loads the verse through its existing Quran
reader, which uses the QuranEnc edition shown there under that edition's own terms.

Status: pilot. The text has not been reviewed by a qualified Islamic scholar.
