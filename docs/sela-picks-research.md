# Sela picks: free classics research

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-21  
**Scope:** metadata-only recommendation catalogue for the Sela reading app.

## Curation

Sela picks contains **227 distinct English-language Project Gutenberg editions**, with no missing authors and a stable Gutenberg landing-page link for every record. It is an editorial reading list, not a claim that these are the exact current positions 1–227 in a popularity ranking.

The selection combines classics and works surfaced by Project Gutenberg's [Best Books Ever](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/bookshelf/13) and [Harvard Classics](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/bookshelf/40) shelves with a current check of its Top 100. The themes are meant to fit Sela's literary, music, memory and homecoming sensibility, while retaining widely read novels, drama, essays, myth, nature writing and biographies. Ten overlapping themes give readers different routes through the same collection; category counts therefore overlap and do not sum to the catalogue total.

The source list started with 32 existing Sela picks. After merging 205 candidate additions, 9 duplicate Gutenberg IDs were deduplicated and one chapter-only Bible record without an individual author was excluded. The final catalogue has 227 unique records: **195 new records** and the 32 existing entries.

## Download behavior and rights

Only catalogue metadata and Gutenberg landing-page URLs are included for the 195 new editions. The full text is requested only after the reader explicitly chooses **Download & add to shelf**. No bulk book download is part of app startup or browsing. Where a selected edition offers more than one suitable format, the app resolves its download URL on demand.

Project Gutenberg states that its public-domain designation applies to the United States and asks readers elsewhere to check local law. Sela links to the work's main Gutenberg page so the user can review available formats and rights before choosing to download. This list should not be treated as jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

## Research sources

- [Project Gutenberg Top 100](https://www.gutenberg.org/browse/scores/top.html) — popularity signal, checked 2026-10-21; rankings change over time.
- [Project Gutenberg offline catalogs and feeds](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/offline_catalogs.html) — machine-readable catalogue source and its recommended access methods.
- [Project Gutenberg terms of use](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/terms_of_use) — source-page linking and service-use constraints, plus the United States public-domain boundary.
- [Gutendex API](https://gutendex.com/) — on-demand metadata and format discovery; not used to trigger downloads before a reader action.
