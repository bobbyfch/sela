Sela is a personal reading space for books, comics and documents. The name means a pause or interval: a small place in the day to return to a story. Sela Books keeps a private library on your device; Sela Viewer brings the same reading experience to existing websites.

## Features

- Read PDF, EPUB, CBZ, TXT, Markdown, HTML, FB2, DOCX and ODT. Optional DjVu support uses an external decoder.
- Choose book, single-page, manga RTL or continuous webtoon reading.
- Keep bookmarks, notes, highlights, reading progress and collections in a local-first library with backup and restore.
- Explore public and free catalogs, browse genres, discover Open Library reader trends and hidden gems, preview paid books, and follow shop links when you want to buy a copy. A lazy, on-device Orama index understands common Indonesian phrasing when searching your own shelf; that local search does not upload the query.
- Fill Home with a quote collection, topic headlines, optional sourced scripture readings, and a localized “on this day” feed; Indonesian headlines fall back to ANTARA when the primary feed fails.
- Track reading time and streaks locally, with a weekly, monthly, or yearly progress view; sessions pause when the reader is not visible.
- Personalize Home with responsive companion artwork, weather-aware scenes, a reading calendar, sourced quotes and optional habit widgets. Islamic users can choose to request estimated prayer times using the saved weather location; coordinates are sent to the calculation provider only after tapping the request button.
- Choose from 18 palettes: Sela, Pelangi, Neon, Rimba, Sakura, Samudra, Lembayung, Kenanga, Senja, Pesisir, Hujan, Seroja, Nila, Embun, Purnama, Arunika, Selat and Jingga. Light, dark and OLED appearance are separate.
- Navigate the web app through five areas: Home, Shelf, Explore, Progress and Settings.
- Use Sela Books as an installable web app, or embed the framework-independent Viewer in HTML, React, Vue, Svelte, Astro, PHP and other sites.
- Interface languages include English, Indonesian, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Chinese and Korean.

## Brand

Sela (pronounced *seh-lah*) means a pause: an unhurried moment to make room for reading. Its reading-space illustrations feature an original mage character and small bookish creatures, with color and atmosphere that respond to the selected appearance, time of day, and available weather. Sela Books is the personal library; Sela Viewer is its embeddable reading companion.
